# NyayaSetu (न्याय सेतु)

AI legal assistant for India's new criminal laws: the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik
Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), which replaced the IPC, CrPC and Indian Evidence
Act on 1 July 2024.

- **AI Legal Assistant:** answers in English, Hindi or Hinglish, with every cited section verified.
- **Case Analyzer:** a trained Section Predictor (TF-IDF + logistic regression on 42,750 IL-TUR case facts),
  fact rules and Groq together give likely charges, punishment and bail, and the user's options.
- **IPC → BNS Converter**, **Document Analyzer** for PDFs, **Law Library**, **Knowledge Graph** and **Evaluation Lab**.

Sign in with the demo account **demo@nyayasetu.in / demo1234**.
